ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m the Artist Behind Bunny Struggles!

Welcome to my little corner of the internet! I’m an artist who loves combining kawaii, anime, and my love for bunnies into cute designs made to brighten your day.

What started as a love for drawing and collecting cute things slowly grew into my small creative shop. I create everything from stickers and stationery to keychains, bookmarks, and other fun goodies, with my bunny characters making their way into many of my designs.

I’m always creating, experimenting, and finding new ways to turn the things I love into something you can enjoy too.

Thank you for stopping by and supporting my small art business—it truly means so much to me!
